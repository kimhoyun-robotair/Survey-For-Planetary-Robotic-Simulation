# Survey project page design

An English academic project page and literature database, with a restrained space and lunar palette.

- Hero and navigation: deep space `#101b2b`, with a subtle slate gradient and lunar silver `#dce2eb` text and primary action.
- Reading surfaces: cool white `#f7f8fa`, lunar gray `#edf0f5`, and white records. Ink `#202b3c`, muted text `#566478`, borders `#d6dce5`, and blue accent `#344f78` provide a consistent hierarchy.
- Status colors: muted blue for direct access, regolith amber `#7b5b27` on `#f3eddf` for request-based access, and neutral gray for unlisted releases. Text labels carry all status meaning.
- Typography: local Arial/Helvetica for text and controls; Georgia for the complete paper title and section titles. No external fonts.
- Opening hierarchy: conference, complete title, authors, shared affiliation, corresponding author, Paper and database actions, abstract, and complete Figure 5 with caption and full-resolution access.
- Layout: 1180px maximum content width, 32px desktop gutters, 20px mobile gutters. The abstract uses an 880px reading width.
- Components: native expandable records, labeled search and filters, source links, methodology disclosures, and an expandable link register.
- Responsive behavior: the hero reflows naturally; columns collapse below 680px; intermediate layouts adapt below 1000px. Wide verification tables scroll within their own region. The original figure keeps its aspect ratio and offers full-size viewing for its fine text.
- Accessibility: semantic landmarks, skip link, visible keyboard focus, named form controls, native keyboard-operable disclosures, live result counts, descriptive image alternative text, and reduced-motion support.
- Source fidelity: the abstract and Figure 5 come from the supplied PDF. The figure retains its original colors, annotations, and explanation blocks. No generated replacement or decorative emoji is used.

The paper’s data and additional literature remain distinct. Resource responses never overwrite the paper’s classifications. All records and source links remain readable without JavaScript.
